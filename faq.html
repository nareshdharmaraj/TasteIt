Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions organized by category

Ordering

How do I place an order?
Simply browse our menu, select your desired items, add them to cart, and proceed to checkout. You can customize each dish and add special instructions.
Can I modify my order after placing it?
You can modify your order within 5 minutes of placing it. After that, please contact our support team for assistance.
What is the minimum order amount?
The minimum order amount is $15 for delivery orders. There's no minimum for pickup orders.
Can I schedule an order for later?
Yes! You can schedule orders up to 7 days in advance. Select your preferred date and time during checkout.
Do you accept bulk orders?
Yes, we accept bulk orders for events and parties. Please contact us at least 48 hours in advance for orders over $200.

Delivery

What are your delivery hours?
We deliver from 11 AM to 10 PM on weekdays and 11 AM to 11 PM on weekends. Orders placed after hours will be delivered the next day.
How long does delivery take?
Standard delivery takes 30-45 minutes. During peak hours, it may take up to 60 minutes. You'll receive real-time tracking updates.
What is the delivery fee?
Delivery fee is $5 for orders under $30, and free for orders above $30. Premium locations may have additional charges.
Do you deliver to my area?
We deliver within a 10-mile radius of our restaurant. Enter your address during checkout to verify delivery availability.
Can I track my order?
Yes! You'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email. You can also track your order in real-time from your dashboard.
What if my order is late?
If your order is more than 15 minutes late, contact our support team. We'll provide a discount on your next order as compensation.

Payment

What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and cash on delivery.
Is my payment information secure?
Yes, we use industry-standard SSL encryption and PCI-compliant payment processing. Your payment information is never stored on our servers.
Can I get a refund?
Full refunds are available for cancelled orders (within 5 minutes) or if there's an issue with your order. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.
Do you offer discounts or promotions?
Yes! Check our special offers section, sign up for our newsletter, and follow us on social media for exclusive deals and promotions.
Can I use multiple payment methods?
Currently, we only support one payment method per order. You can use reward points in combination with any payment method.

Menu & Food

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options?
Yes! We have extensive vegetarian options and several vegan dishes. Look for the "Vegetarian" badge on our menu items.
How spicy are your dishes?
Each dish has a spicy level indicator (🌶️) from 0-4. You can also request to adjust the spice level when ordering. Just add a note with your preference!
Can I customize my order?
Absolutely! You can add special instructions for any dish. We'll do our best to accommodate your preferences, though some modifications may incur additional charges.
Do you have gluten-free options?
Yes, we offer several gluten-free dishes. Look for items marked with the "Gluten-Free" badge or contact us for recommendations.
Are your ingredients fresh and locally sourced?
We prioritize fresh, high-quality ingredients and source locally whenever possible. Our produce is delivered daily, and we never use frozen meats.
Do you provide nutritional information?
Yes, nutritional information is available for all menu items. Click on any dish to view detailed calorie, protein, carb, and fat content.

Account

Do I need an account to order?
You can order as a guest, but creating an account gives you access to order history, saved addresses, reward points, and exclusive offers.
Do you have a loyalty program?
Yes! Create an account to earn points with every order. Points can be redeemed for discounts and free items. You also get exclusive access to special offers and early menu releases.
How do I reset my password?
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and we'll send you a reset link. The link expires after 24 hours for security.
Can I save multiple delivery addresses?
Yes, you can save unlimited delivery addresses in your account. Label them as "Home," "Work," etc. for easy selection during checkout.
How do I delete my account?
Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account. Note that this action is permanent and will delete all your order history and reward points.
Can I link my social media accounts?
Yes, you can sign up and log in using Facebook, Google, or Apple. This makes ordering faster and you won't need to remember another password.
